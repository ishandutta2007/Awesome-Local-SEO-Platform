# Awesome-Local-SEO-Platform

## Top Local SEO Platforms Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on Local Rank Tracking, Citations, Google Business Profile, Listings Management & Map Pack Visibility*

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Local SEO**. These tools help businesses and agencies manage local search visibility—citations/NAP consistency, Google Business Profile optimization, local pack/geo-grid rank tracking, reviews, and multi-location listing distribution.



**Examples** include BrightLocal, Whitespark, Yext, Rio SEO, Uberall, Semrush Local, Moz Local, Synup, Local Falcon, and GeoRanker (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Full local SEO suites (listings networks + citations + geo-grid + reputation) are largely commercial. Strong open options exist for **self-hosted rank tracking** (SerpBear and similar), **Google Maps geo-grid checkers**, and general SEO toolkits. This section lists the most practical open resources and is realistic about the gap.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[BrightLocal](https://www.brightlocal.com/)**  

  All-in-one local SEO platform popular with agencies for citation building, rank tracking, reputation monitoring, and white-label reporting.



- **[Whitespark](https://whitespark.ca/)**  

  Local SEO toolkit known for thorough citation discovery, local rank tracking, and review management tools.



- **[Yext](https://www.yext.com/)**  

  Enterprise listings and knowledge management platform for large multi-location brands, with broad publisher distribution and real-time control.



- **[Rio SEO](https://www.rioseo.com/)**  

  Enterprise local SEO and location marketing platform focused on multi-location brands and franchise networks.



- **[Uberall](https://uberall.com/)**  

  Location marketing platform supporting listings, reviews, and local presence management at scale.



- **[Semrush Local](https://www.semrush.com/)**  

  Local SEO capabilities within the Semrush suite, including listing management and local rank insights for existing Semrush users.



- **[Moz Local](https://moz.com/products/local)**  

  Listing distribution and local presence tool from Moz for submitting and syncing business data across core directories.



- **[Synup](https://synup.com/)**  

  Local listings and reputation management platform aimed at agencies and multi-location businesses.



- **[Local Falcon](https://www.localfalcon.com/)**  

  Geo-grid local rank tracking tool specializing in heatmap-style Map Pack visibility across a service area.



- **[GeoRanker](https://www.georanker.com/)**  

  Local and traditional rank tracking platform with geo-specific SERP and local pack monitoring capabilities.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[SerpBear](https://github.com/towfiqi/serpbear)**  

  Open-source, self-hosted rank tracker. Track unlimited keywords and domains; you supply SERP data via scrapers or third-party APIs.



- **[OpenSEO and self-hosted SEO toolkits](https://github.com/)**  

  Open all-in-one SEO platforms supporting rank tracking, audits, and related workflows (often BYOK for SERP/data APIs).



- **[Google Maps / GBP geo-grid rank trackers](https://github.com/)**  

  Open-source tools that check Google Maps rankings across a geographic grid and produce heatmaps of local pack visibility.



- **[Senkiu and DataForSEO-based open trackers](https://github.com/)**  

  Self-hosted rank tracking and site explorer tools built on top of commercial SERP APIs with cost accounting.



- **[Open-source rank tracker comparisons and lists](https://github.com/)**  

  Maintained catalogs of self-hosted SEO rank trackers with licenses, data sources, and project health notes.



- **[Citation and NAP audit open scripts](https://github.com/)**  

  Community scripts for crawling directories and checking name/address/phone consistency (manual or semi-automated).



- **[Google Business Profile open helpers](https://github.com/)**  

  Utilities and unofficial tools for interacting with GBP data and insights (subject to Google API and ToS constraints).



- **[Review monitoring open prototypes](https://github.com/)**  

  Lightweight open tools for aggregating and alerting on Google and other local reviews.



- **[Local landing page and schema open generators](https://github.com/)**  

  Templates and scripts for generating location pages and LocalBusiness structured data.



- **[SERP scraping and normalization open libraries](https://github.com/)**  

  Open APIs and CLIs that normalize local and organic SERP results from multiple search engines.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- Starting with **SerpBear** or similar self-hosted rank trackers when you want full control over keyword monitoring costs and data.

- Using open geo-grid Maps checkers for detailed local pack heatmaps without a commercial geo-grid subscription.

- Combining open rank tracking with commercial listing networks (or manual citations) for a hybrid stack.

- Accepting that large publisher distribution networks, automated citation building at scale, enterprise multi-location governance, and polished agency reporting still favor commercial platforms (BrightLocal, Yext, Whitespark, Uberall, Moz Local, Local Falcon, etc.).

- Focusing open-source efforts on transparent rank data and privacy-preserving local monitoring.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Track rankings with SerpBear or a geo-grid tool → audit citations with scripts or commercial finders → manage core listings via a commercial network or manual process → monitor reviews → publish consistent NAP and location pages. Suitable for technical SEOs and agencies comfortable managing APIs and hosting. Most multi-location brands continue to rely on commercial local SEO platforms for distribution and support.
